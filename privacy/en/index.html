Praca Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026.

Publisher: Shockwave93. Privacy contact: hubertmolek@gmail.com.

This policy describes the current version of Praca. When you contact the publisher by email, your sender address, message and any attachments are delivered to the publisher's mailbox to handle your enquiry. The publisher does not have remote access to the private journal on your phone.

Journal, backups and sharing

Praca is a work time journal. It stores work and driving entries, dates and times, projects, places, descriptions, notes, settings and change history in the app's private storage. The current version does not require an account and has no dedicated synchronisation service, advertising, analytics or automatic crash reporting. Manual entry works without the internet; optional maps and system services are described below.

You can save a journal backup or export to a location you choose and share it with an app you choose. A backup contains the journal and its history, including retained place information and photo references; photo originals have a separate backup. Praca does not add encryption to these files. Access depends on the selected location and receiving app; a folder you select may be managed by an online service under that service's own terms. Automatic backups are saved to a previously selected location when you enable this feature. Older recognised automatic backups may be deleted by rotation; manual backups and unrelated files are excluded.

Photos and their embedded information

You can take a photo using the system camera or deliberately attach an existing photo. The app requests the access needed when you use the feature; the system may provide access only to selected photos. Denying access does not block the journal.

Praca does not automatically scan the entire gallery. Accessible photos may contain a date, camera information and a location recorded by the camera. The system may restrict access to this information; the app does not bypass those restrictions. If coordinates are available, the app may show the place and ask a system service for an address, as described below. Retained originals may still include this information when shared outside Praca.

By default, new photos on newer devices are visible in the phone's gallery; you can choose the app's private storage instead. Existing photos may remain in their selected source. Links, thumbnails and the information needed to display them are stored locally. Changing where new photos are saved does not automatically move earlier photos. Photos are not sent to Immich or a Praca service. Uploading to your own Google Drive requires connecting your account and separately enabling photos, as described below.

Unlinking a photo from a day, deleting a work segment or resetting a day does not delete the original. Separately deleting a photo from the library requires confirmation; the app deletes the local file when it has access and no other retained asset uses it. Android may require additional consent. Earlier backups and files in other services are not automatically deleted. After access is lost or a file is deleted, its link and an indication that the photo is unavailable may remain.

Deleting a photo in the system gallery may move it to the gallery's trash, where the original remains until later deletion. Praca marks the photo as being in the trash and retains its link to the day; an auxiliary preview does not replace an accessible original. Restoring the photo in the gallery makes the same photo available after the app refreshes. Explicitly deleting its entry in Praca remains possible after the original has been confirmed missing; this does not delete earlier separate backups.

Retention and deletion

The journal and its history have no automatic expiry date. Deleting entries and resetting days does not erase their earlier versions from the history or existing backups. Local diagnostic files are rotated, and auxiliary data such as thumbnails, addresses and map images may be deleted and regenerated. Diagnostic export is manual; it should not contain the contents of notes, descriptions or project names. Zone diagnostics are described below.

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes the private journal and private files from that device. Photos in the gallery, manually saved exports and separate backups may remain; you delete them where they are stored. Praca cannot delete copies shared with other apps. Revoking location or photo permission restricts future access but does not automatically delete previously stored history and backups.

Optional zones and location

Praca stores the journal locally. Zone automation is off by default. You define places using “Set here”, a map selection or entered coordinates, together with radii, days and an optional project; you can later change or delete zone definitions.

“Set here” uses precise location once. Automation uses system entry and exit events even after the app is closed, following separate consent for background location. Praca does not record continuous route samples. System location services are subject to Android settings; “Set here” is independent of the map.

The journal retains the centre coordinates and radius of defined zones, their changes in the history, entry/exit/dwell events with receipt time and outcome, and the work and driving entries created. Events do not store an additional phone position. A received signal waits locally in a private queue until its storage is acknowledged; this queue is not included in backups and contains no phone position. The Android library may temporarily retain the triggering position in its event handling queue; that queue is not part of the journal, its backups or diagnostic exports. Zone definitions, history, stored events and work and driving entries are included in journal backups that you deliberately create and share; Android permissions and registrations are not transferred through backups.

By default, data is not deleted after a set period. “Delete zone events” in settings removes the event records; it does not delete work, driving, zone definitions, their history or earlier backups. Deleting a zone definition stops its future use; earlier entries and history remain. You delete your own backups separately where they are stored.

Denying or revoking consent, disabling location and battery restrictions may stop automation. Manual entry continues to work. You can change permissions in system settings. If an exit delay is enabled, an entry and notification may appear only after a later event or after opening the app, using the original exit time.

Zone diagnostics locally record the time, type and short zone identifier, transition, revision, registration source and automation decision. From the position supplied by Android, they calculate only the distance from the centre in metres; they do not record coordinates or a place name. The library in use does not expose position accuracy or the initial-event flag, so the log explicitly records these omissions and the classification of the brief settling period after registration. This data is included in manually exported diagnostics; it is not sent automatically. Registration metadata and pending entries remain outside journal backups.

Map for defining zones

The map is optional and downloads images of the viewed area from the OpenStreetMap Foundation server (tile.openstreetmap.org), only while the map view is open. OSMF receives the IP address, the Praca app identifier and tile numbers that reveal the viewed area and zoom level. We do not send the zone name, project, journal, entry/exit events or the phone's route; the centre you select is not a separate server request, but the viewed area may reveal it. The OSMF Privacy Policy sets out the provider's practices. Tapping attribution opens the OSM copyright page in an external browser at your request.

Repeat views use a local map image cache according to server headers; the cache is outside the journal and its backups, has a soft limit of 50 MiB checked at initialisation, and may be removed by the system or by clearing the app's cache. We do not pre-download areas. A network error or timeout replaces the map with a coordinate entry field; entering coordinates requires neither the internet nor location permission. The manual journal works fully offline.

The internet connection is used to download zone map images and the day's place preview. Address search and address lookup are delegated to system services. After you explicitly connect an account, the connection is also used for Google authorisation and synchronisation with your own Google Drive. The journal continues to work without an account or internet access.

“Search address” invokes the system Android Geocoder only after you submit a query. The system geocoding provider receives the query text and language and may send them to its online service according to the device's settings and policies. Praca does not select an additional search server. We show at most five results; queries and address labels stay in the screen's memory and are not stored in the journal, backups, cache or logs. Saving a zone stores only its existing centre coordinates. Lack of a network or service does not block coordinate entry.

The day's place and map

The day card shows a separate place line above the description; it adds nothing to your text. It uses historical centres of work zones assigned to work or, if there is no such zone, stored coordinates from that day's photos. It does not request a new phone position. Editing or deleting the current zone definition does not change the place of an earlier assignment. Deleting zone events does not remove the place retained in the journal's history.

Opening a card with a place sends the coordinates of the first place and the language to the system Android Geocoder to obtain a street and locality; expanding the place list may look up addresses for visible rows. The system provider may use its network. Addresses are stored locally as auxiliary data by point, language and journal identity; after a restart they can be read without a network. They are not included in the description, journal, backups or logs. After restoring a backup they must be looked up again. A missing address does not block the manual journal.

The small map preview of the first place uses the same OSMF server and cache as the zone map. OSMF receives the IP address, app identifier and tile numbers revealing the viewed area; it does not receive the description, the day's date, place name or photo. Downloading runs only for the visible preview on the current card; scrolling it off screen, switching views, expanding the list or moving the app to the background closes connections. There is no pre-downloading, route tracking or new location permission. A map error leaves a short message; an address already looked up remains available.

Optional synchronisation with Google Drive

When you choose “Connect with Google”, Praca requests permission to save and read its own files in the visible Praca folder on your Google Drive. Access is limited to files used by the app; Praca does not request access to your entire Drive. The purpose is to move your journal between devices and keep photo copies. The transferred journal includes work and driving entries, projects, places and stored coordinates, descriptions, notes, settings, photo links and complete change history. The publisher does not gain access to your account or files.

By default, only the journal is synchronised. Separately enabling photos shows their count and size. It covers only photos attached to Praca days, without browsing or uploading your entire gallery. Original bytes are copied, including available date, device and location information stored in the photo. By default, photos transfer only over Wi-Fi; the journal may use any network. Connection state is checked only for these conditions; we do not read your Wi-Fi name or network location.

Praca reads the account identifier and email address to distinguish connections and display your chosen account. It does not read your Google password. System Google services manage access; credentials are not part of the journal, its backups or exports. Manual attempts and enabled daily attempts may send and receive changed data after the app is closed. Android may delay a task, and reopening the app catches up. Restoring a backup or changing accounts pauses synchronisation until you explicitly reconnect.

Disconnecting stops subsequent attempts and keeps the local journal. Files already uploaded remain on your Drive until you delete them there; you can also revoke app access in your Google Account settings. Deleting an entry or photo in Praca retains earlier history and does not automatically delete previously uploaded files. Removing Drive data does not delete local facts, but may stop synchronisation that needs the missing data to be recovered.

Connections to Google are encrypted in transit. Praca does not add its own encryption to Drive files; visibility and retention also depend on your settings and Google’s privacy policy. Data received from Google is used for the described journal and synchronisation features, without advertising, analytics, selling data or training models. Google data use is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Data protection and the policy page

Private app data is protected by Android's access controls. Photos in the gallery, selected backup folders and deliberately shared files are also subject to the permissions and policies of those locations. Praca does not add encryption to backups or exports.
